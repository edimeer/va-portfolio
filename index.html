About me

New to VA work. Not new to work.

That's the work I want to do now: sit next to a business owner, take the busywork off their desk, and fix the process behind it so it doesn't come back. I'm building this practice on purpose. It isn't a stopgap between jobs.

FAQ

Common questions

You're new to VA work. Why should I hire you?

Fair question. I'm new to the title, not to the work. For seven years I triaged issues, wrote documentation, managed tasks in Jira and kept remote teams updated, which is most of what a good VA does. On top of that I can automate and fix things most VAs would have to pass on. If you'd like to see it first, the trial task lets you judge my work before you commit.

What are your rates?

It depends on the mix of work and how many hours a week you need. We'll agree on a clear rate on the intro call, in writing, before any work starts. No surprises on the invoice.

How do you keep my accounts and data safe?

I never ask for passwords by email or chat. I prefer my own user login on your tools, or access shared through a password manager such as 1Password, Bitwarden or LastPass, so you can revoke it at any time. I use two-factor authentication on every account, only ask for the access a task needs, and I'm happy to sign an NDA before we start.

Can you handle both admin and technical tasks?

Yes, that's the point. I can run your inbox and calendar one day and fix a website issue or build an automation the next, so you don't need to hire separate people.

How do we work together day to day?

We agree on a channel such as Slack or email and the hours I'm online. At the end of each working day I send a short written update covering what's done, what's next and anything I need from you.

Can we start small?

Yes. We begin with a small, clearly scoped trial task so we can both see if it's a good fit before agreeing on regular hours.

Which time zones can you cover?

I'm in the Philippines (UTC+8) and work 4 to 6 hours a day, Monday to Friday. We'll set those hours to overlap with your business day, whether you're in the US, UK, Europe or Australia.
